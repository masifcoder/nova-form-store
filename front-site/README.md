# NOVA FORM - Front-Site E-Commerce Website

## Overview
A luxury men's footwear storefront prototype tailored for the Pakistani market, showcasing 24 authentic handcrafted footwear models (Oxfords, Penny Loafers, Chelsea Boots, Peshawari Chappals, Sneakers, and Sandals).

## Technology Stack
- **HTML5:** Semantic, accessible markup.
- **CSS3:** Custom styles (`assets/css/style.css`, `assets/css/responsive.css`).
- **Bootstrap 5.3.3:** Responsive grid, navbar, offcanvas, dropdowns, and modals.
- **Bootstrap Icons 1.11.3:** Vector icons.
- **Google Fonts:** Plus Jakarta Sans & Playfair Display.
- **Minimal Vanilla JavaScript:** (`assets/js/main.js`) for cart counter, thumbnail switcher, quantity toggles, live cart calculations, coupon codes (`NOVA10`), and demo modals.

## Pages
1. `index.php` - Homepage with hero, categories, best sellers, promo banner, new arrivals, brand benefits, reviews, and newsletter.
2. `shop.php` - Complete product catalog with search, category filtering, price filter, sorting, and pagination.
3. `category.php` - Curated category listing for Formal Shoes & Loafers.
4. `product-details.php` - Product details with interactive image gallery, size pills (EU 39-46), colors, tabs, and related items.
5. `cart.php` - Shopping bag with dynamic subtotal, coupon discount, and free shipping calculation.
6. `checkout.php` - Checkout with Pakistani addresses, Cash on Delivery, Bank Transfer, and demo order confirmation modal.
7. `login.php` - Client login.
8. `register.php` - Client registration.
9. `forgot-password.php` - Password reset demo.
10. `account.php` - Customer account dashboard.
11. `orders.php` - Order history table.
12. `order-details.php` - Detailed order status timeline and items.
13. `wishlist.php` - Saved footwear models.
14. `about.php` - Brand heritage and Goodyear-welted philosophy.
15. `contact.php` - Contact form and store ateliers in Lahore, Karachi, and Islamabad.
16. `size-guide.php` - EU 39-46 size conversion table with centimeters and measurement instructions.
